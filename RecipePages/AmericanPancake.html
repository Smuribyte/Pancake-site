INGREDIENTS

  • 1½ cups (190 g) all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
  • 1½ teaspoons baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 1¼ cups (300 ml) buttermilk
  • 1 large egg
  • 3 tablespoons (42 g) melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Extra butter or cooking oil for the pan

INSTRUCTIONS

  1. Mix the dry ingredients

    In a large bowl, combine the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.

  2. Mix the wet ingredients

    In another bowl, whisk together the buttermilk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla extract.

  3. Combine

    Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients. Gently whisk or fold until combined. Do not overmix; a few small lumps are fine.

  4. Heat the pan

    Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium-low to medium heat. Lightly grease it with butter or oil.

  5. Cook the pancakes

    Pour approximately ¼ cup of batter onto the pan for each pancake. Cook until bubbles appear on the surface and the edges begin to look set, approximately 2–3 minutes.

  6. Flip

    Flip the pancake and cook the other side for approximately 1–2 minutes, until golden brown.

  7. Serve

    Stack the pancakes and serve immediately. Typical toppings include butter and maple syrup.

Buttermilk Substitute

If you don't have buttermilk, use:

  • 1¼ cups (300 ml) regular milk
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice or white vinegar

Mix the milk and lemon juice or vinegar together and let it sit for 5–10 minutes before using.
